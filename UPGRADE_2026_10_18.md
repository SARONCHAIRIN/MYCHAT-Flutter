## Critical backend limitation
The source itself documents that message history may omit attachment metadata and `/uploads/...` may not be publicly served. A local preview cannot make media persist across devices or after app reinstall. Backend needs to return durable attachment metadata with message history and serve authenticated/downloadable media. Do not label this issue resolved until verified against real server responses.
